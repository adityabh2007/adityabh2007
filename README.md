### 🏗️ Infradev — *Find the fault before it finds us.*
`AI` · `Robotics` · `Structural Safety` · **WRO India 2025 · National-level project**

**Infradev** is a rover-and-drone system that spots structural cracks early, so repairs can happen before small failures become major accidents. Built with **Sarvagya** for the WRO India senior category.

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 How it works</b></summary>
<br/>

| Step | What happens |
|:---:|:---|
| **01** | Ultrasonic sensors and a rotating **LiDAR** scan surfaces for gaps and irregular reflections. |
| **02** | An **AI-enabled camera** captures suspected cracks for image-based analysis in **Python**. |
| **03** | A **rover** handles ground inspections; a **drone** carries the same monitoring approach to high-rise structures. |

**🏅 Result:** Scored **138.75** at the WRO India regional round (Manav Rachna University) and earned a place at the **national level in Hyderabad** — one of only **six** senior-category projects to advance from Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Jharkhand and Bihar.

</details>
